
# fedo

**In-app feedback, voting, and roadmaps for mobile apps.**

One method call opens a polished feedback screen inside your app. Users post
ideas and bugs, upvote what matters, and follow status from Open to Shipped.
Everything lands in your dashboard. No UI to build, no backend to maintain.

[Documentation](https://docs.getfedo.com) · [getfedo.com](https://getfedo.com) · [Pricing](https://getfedo.com/pricing)

### What we collect

Only what your users explicitly submit — feedback text, plus any metadata you
choose to attach. No passive tracking, no fingerprinting, no ad identifiers.
The SDK source is public so you can verify that.
